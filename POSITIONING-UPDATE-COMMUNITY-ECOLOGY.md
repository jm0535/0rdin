## 📋 Rationale

Ördin's capabilities extend **beyond biodiversity alone** to encompass the full scope of community ecology:

### What Community Ecology Includes

1. **Diversity Analysis**
   - Species diversity (Shannon, Simpson, etc.)
   - Diversity estimation and rarefaction (iNEXT)
   - Hill numbers (q=0, 1, 2)
   - Evenness indices (Pielou, Simpson's E, Evar)

2. **Ordination Analysis**
   - NMDS, PCA, CA, DCA, PCoA
   - Community composition patterns
   - Similarity/dissimilarity analysis
   - Beta diversity visualization

3. **Community Structure**
   - Species abundance distributions
   - Community composition
   - Assemblage patterns
   - Ecological gradients

4. **Future Capabilities** (v2.4+)
   - Beta diversity partitioning
   - Hierarchical clustering
   - Hypothesis testing (PERMANOVA, ANOSIM)
   - Environmental correlations

## 🎓 Why "Community Ecology"?

### Broader Scope

**Biodiversity** focuses on:
- Number of species
- Species richness
- Diversity indices

**Community Ecology** encompasses:
- ✅ Biodiversity (species diversity)
- ✅ Species interactions
- ✅ Community structure
- ✅ Assembly patterns
- ✅ Succession
- ✅ Spatial patterns
- ✅ Temporal dynamics
- ✅ Environmental correlations

### Alignment with vegan Package

The `vegan` package is explicitly a **"Community Ecology Package"** according to its official documentation. Since Ördin heavily integrates vegan (19 functions in v2.2.0), positioning as a community ecology platform is accurate.

### Future-Proof

As Ördin expands to include:
- Beta diversity partitioning
- Null models
- Nestedness analysis
- Network analysis
- Functional diversity

The "community ecology" positioning naturally accommodates these features, while "biodiversity" would be limiting.
